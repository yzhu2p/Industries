C45 Series Flat Belt Conveyor

Delivering Reliable, Custom-Fit Flat Belt Conveyors Built for Industrial Performance

Request a quote

Engineered for Efficiency

At Proax, our flat belt conveyor systems are designed for installation flexibility and optimal performance. Whether your application demands high speeds, side-load tracking, or space-saving configurations, our C45 Series delivers reliable, custom solutions for your facility.

Key Features

Application

Ideal for light and medium-duty operations

Load Capacity

Handles up to 225 kg (496 lbs)

Speed Range

Reaches up to 98 m/min (320 ft/min)

Available Sizes

Width: 90 to 610 mm (3.5"–24")
Length: up to 6000 mm (236")

Frame Width Options

Versatile sizing options ensure the perfect fit for your application. Seven standard frame widths accommodate everything from compact workstations to large-scale production lines.

Drive Options

  • Left or Right Drive for installation flexibility
  • Gear Motor could be mounted parallel or 90 degrees
  • Typical drive direction will be pulling the product

Belt Tracking Options

  • Crowned roller on all sizes
  • V-Guided roller option from 203mm (8") for side load
  • Easier to adjust when need it
  • Could run faster without tracking issue

Belt Options

PVC Belt

Cost-effective general purpose belt material ideal for light to medium duty applications and easy to clean.

TPU Belt

High durability belt with excellent wear resistance and chemical resistance, suitable for heavy-duty industrial use.

Motor Options

Input Voltage

Supports various voltages

AC or DC Motor

Up to 400W gear motors (5:1 to 30:1 ratio)

Constant & Variable Speed

Optional AC drive

Support Options

Levelling feet

Levelling feet with various heights available.

Casters

Casters available with multiple profile options, fixed and swivel wheels, plus optional lock wheels.

Side Guides

Available with both fixed and adjustable guide options.
